Migrating to **AudioLDM-2** would likely improve several aspects of your output, particularly the **audio fidelity** and **artifacting**, but it may not be the "silver bullet" for melodic catchiness. As a Solutions Architect, you'll appreciate the architectural trade-offs: `MusicGen-small` is an autoregressive Transformer (300M parameters) optimized for speed, while `AudioLDM-2` is a Latent Diffusion Model (LDM) that handles a broader semantic "language of audio".

Here is how the migration addresses your specific goals:

### 1. Removing Clipping and Static
* **The Benefit:** `AudioLDM-2` generally exhibits less digital clipping than the `MusicGen-small` model. `MusicGen` relies on the **EnCodec** decoder, which often struggles with high-frequency "noise" descriptions (like your "laughter" or "loose change"), leading to low-frequency distortions that ripple into high-frequency artifacts.
* **Negative Prompting:** Unlike `MusicGen`, `AudioLDM-2` supports **negative prompts**. You can explicitly exclude "static," "white noise," and "clipping" from the generation process to clean up the signal.

### 2. Ensuring a Coherent Melody
* **The Architecture Gap:** While `AudioLDM-2` produces higher-fidelity sound (up to **48kHz** vs. `MusicGen`'s standard **32kHz**), it is a general audio model. It treats music, speech, and sound effects through a shared representation. 
* **The Risk:** In some benchmarks, `MusicGen` is actually rated higher for **musical structure** because it was trained exclusively on a vast library of music. `AudioLDM-2` can sometimes lean too far into "soundscape" territory, producing atmospheric textures instead of a defined, verse-chorus melody.
* **The Better Alternative:** If melody is your primary concern, migrating to **`facebook/musicgen-melody`** (1.5B parameters) may be more effective than switching families. The "melody" variant allows you to provide a reference audio file to guide the structure, which essentially guarantees a coherent lead.

| Feature | MusicGen-small | AudioLDM-2 |

### Recommendation
Migrating to `AudioLDM-2` will solve your **technical audio quality** issues (static/clipping) because its diffusion process is better at filtering out the "noise" generated by your descriptive prompt. 

However, to achieve the "catchy" and "melodic" goals, you should also consider:
1.  **Switching to `musicgen-medium` or `large`:** The increased parameter count (1.5B - 3.3B) significantly reduces hallucinated noise and improves melodic complexity.
2.  **Using SOTA Models (2025/2026):** Since it is currently 2026, newer architectures like **ACE-Step** or **MMGen** have surpassed `AudioLDM-2` in melodic coherence by using dedicated alignment modules to sync text and harmony.

**Verdict:** Yes, migrate for the cleaner signal, but stick to **`audioldm2-music-665k`** specifically for the best musical results.
